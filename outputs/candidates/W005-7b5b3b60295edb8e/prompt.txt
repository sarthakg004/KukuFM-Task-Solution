WHAT COUNTS AS A GOOD PROMO
1. Hook: A compelling line or visible action starts within the first 3 seconds of
   the selected clip. Explain the actual event, not just that it is "dramatic."
   Do not rely on an attention-grabbing line that appears later in the excerpt.
2. Clarity: The selected footage itself establishes who is in conflict and what
   is at stake. A viewer must not need the logline, continuity notes, an earlier
   episode, captions or added narration to understand the essential situation.
3. Escalation: The middle develops the conflict rather than repeating insults,
   reactions or information the opening has already delivered.
4. Cliffhanger: Stop on a specific unresolved choice, threat, confrontation or
   discovery that creates a reason to watch what follows. A sad face, an insult,
   a musical swell or a cut to black alone is not a cliffhanger. A reveal can
   open a new question, but do not include the answer to your claimed question.
5. Duration: Total selected footage must be between 20 and 60 seconds inclusive.

BOUNDARIES AND SEGMENTS
- Prefer one continuous excerpt when it works. Combine a few segments only if
  the result remains understandable and gains something over a continuous cut.
  Segments may come from different episodes. Their list is playback order.
- Use exact source IDs. start_id supplies that entry's START timestamp; end_id
  supplies that entry's END timestamp. Both IDs must belong to the same episode
  for each segment. One ID may supply both boundaries if its duration is suitable.
- A segment includes ALL original footage between its boundaries. Selecting two
  dialogue IDs does not remove the intervening camera shots, pauses or dialogue.
  Audiovisual entries overlap and remain in source order; this is not a playlist
  of individual screenplay entries.
- Calculate each segment's duration as END minus START, then sum the segments.
  Do not sum entry durations. Do not repeat or overlap footage within a promo.
- Choose clean cuts: avoid cutting a spoken sentence midway, or leaving a
  pronoun/question with no understandable referent. Include essential setup,
  but remove slow introductions and trailing black frames where possible.
- Do not change timestamps, invent source IDs, add footage or manufacture a new
  causal relationship by joining unrelated moments. Avoid warned boundaries.

- standalone_context: Explain what a new viewer can infer from the selected
